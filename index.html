    From Intention To Daily Action

    Three steps, the same every time. You can read the reasoning at each one.

    1. 01

      Say What You Want To Become

      Write it in your own words. A time frame like “in 8 weeks”, “by May” or “this year” sets the horizon. Without one, the plan runs 90 days.

    2. 02

      Get A Dated Plan

      Four milestones spaced evenly to your date, a short list of weekly actions and one daily habit, shaped for career, fitness, learning or creative goals.

    3. 03

      Check In, Get One Next Step

      Each check-in moves your streak and pillars. The coach answers with one message and shows the rule and the numbers it used.
